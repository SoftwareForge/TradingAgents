## Company Overview

AMD is a leading global semiconductor company designing and manufacturing microprocessors, graphics processing units (GPUs), and related technologies. The company competes primarily with Intel in the CPU market and with NVIDIA in the GPU/AI accelerator market. AMD has gained significant market share in both data center and consumer segments through innovative product architectures (Zen, RDNA) and strategic acquisitions (Xilinx, Pensando).
