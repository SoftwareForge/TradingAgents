The Neutral Analyst confirmed that relying on political mandates alone is risky without earnings support. **Decision:** We accept this risk but mitigate it strictly via hedging rather than exiting the position prematurely.

3.  **Execution Protocol:**

The Aggressive and Neutral analysts both agreed that holding core positions with stops at the VWMA ($716) is superior to selling into strength or waiting for a confirmation breakout above $737.22 (Bollinger Upper) without evidence. **Decision:** We enforce this specific risk management layer.
